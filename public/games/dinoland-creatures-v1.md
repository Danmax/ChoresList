# DinoLand creature atlas

`dinoland-creatures-v1.png` is an original 1774 × 887 RGBA atlas made with the built-in imagegen tool. It has four columns and two rows. The cells are, left to right:

1. Triceratops, long-neck sauropod, baboon, saber-toothed tiger
2. Pterodactyl, mammoth, T. rex, volcanic lava rock

Canvas samples exact quarter-width and half-height cells in `lib/jungle-dino-art.ts`. The final image has genuine transparency. Every cell's visible pixels remain inside that cell; measured horizontal gutters are at least 33 px on both sides of every sprite. This allows distinct crop rectangles without neighboring characters bleeding into animations.
