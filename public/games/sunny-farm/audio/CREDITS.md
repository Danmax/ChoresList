# Sunny Farm Helpers audio

Animal clips are local copies of the short, loudness-matched CC0 recordings distributed by Newel Labs at https://newellabs.com/play/animal-farm/sounds/ (downloaded 2026-11-16). Its sound credits and description identify the original Freesound recordings as CC0: https://newellabs.com/play/animal-farm/#credits . No Newel Labs game code or artwork is used.

`click.wav`, `collect.wav`, `bell.wav`, and `success.wav` are original, softly enveloped sine-tone effects created for this project. No samples from paid libraries are used. All playback stays local to this app. There are no streamed ads, trackers, or ongoing audio-provider requests.
